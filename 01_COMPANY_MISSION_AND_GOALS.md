# 01 — COMPANY MISSION & GOAL TREE

## Mission (paste into Paperclip company)
Produce a rigorous, evidence-based blueprint — and later a working prototype — for an open, modular, AI-native clinical research platform that connects hospital data, trial protocols, clinical workflows, data capture, validation and multicenter research into one auditable system. Initial focus: rare-cancer and precision-oncology trials (e.g. glioblastoma). Architecture must stay reusable for other specialties. It should match or exceed the capabilities of the Yamicc Platform while avoiding vendor and low-code lock-in.

## Working product concept
"An open clinical research operating system": AI-assisted, human-verified, standards-based, federated where needed, auditable everywhere.

## Design principles
Open standards · open source where practical · vendor independence · modular deployment · AI automation with human oversight · privacy by design · clinical-grade auditability · multicenter by default · reusable structured data · low deployment and running cost · oncology concepts as modules, not hardcoded in the core.

## Goal tree (create as Paperclip goals; tasks in 09 trace back to these)

- **G0 — Decide what to build and how (the blueprint).**
  - **G1 — Know the competitor.** Verified Yamicc analysis separating announced / demonstrated / piloted / in production. (Deliverable D2)
  - **G2 — Know the landscape.** Commercial, open-source, data sources, standards; capability gap analysis. (D3, D4)
  - **G3 — Know the users.** Roles, jobs-to-be-done, pain points, critical workflows. (D5)
  - **G4 — Specify the product.** Requirements matrix with acceptance criteria and build/reuse decisions; UI/UX spec. (D6, D7)
  - **G5 — Design the system.** Architecture, data & standards strategy, AI architecture. (D8, D9, D10)
  - **G6 — De-risk.** Regulatory, privacy, security and validation strategy. (D11)
  - **G7 — Choose the path.** Build-vs-buy matrix, MVP selection, roadmap, cost ranges, open questions. (D12–D15)
  - **G8 — Final answer.** Executive summary + answer to the strategic question in 08. (D1)
- **G9 — (After human approval of G0) Build the first demo** on synthetic data per the selected MVP.

## Success criteria for G0
- Every important factual claim has a source and an evidence label.
- Every requirement has ID, role, priority, acceptance criteria, existing alternatives and a build/integrate/reuse decision.
- One MVP is recommended with explicit reasoning vs. the alternatives.
- Clear list of what NOT to build.
- Costs given as ranges with stated assumptions; no invented precision.

## Strategic question the whole company must answer
If we started today, with modern AI models, existing open-source healthcare infrastructure and a small but capable team, what is the smartest, fastest, most affordable and most defensible way to build an alternative to Yamicc's envisioned platform? (Full sub-questions in 08.)
